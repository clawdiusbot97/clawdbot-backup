# 📰 Newsletter Intelligence — 2026-05-23

### Theme 1: Enterprise AI governance becomes a feature (not a checkbox)
- Strategic insight: buyers are starting to reward vendors who expose concrete security tooling (threat modeling, vuln disclosure, auditability) as part of the product.
- Tactical opportunity: create a simple “vendor governance checklist” you can reuse (logs, data retention, redaction, evaluation, incident response), then score vendors consistently.

### Theme 2: AI shifts into embedded workflows (Office + Design)
- Strategic insight: distribution (PowerPoint, Figma) is eating model differentiation, and the winners are the tools that sit in the daily loop.
- Tactical opportunity: pick one internal workflow artifact to automate end-to-end (deck draft, design spec, requirements doc) and measure time saved.
