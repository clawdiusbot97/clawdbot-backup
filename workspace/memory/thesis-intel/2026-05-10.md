# Thesis Intel — 2026-05-10

## TL;DR
- Voice AI is pushing hard on sub-second turn-taking (WebRTC stacks, TTFA benchmarks), which matters for broker/claims voice workflows.
- Enterprise AI is packaging “services + deployment” via PE-backed ventures, a signal that implementation/ops is becoming the monetization layer.
- EU AI Act compliance is shifting from “prep” to “enforcement readiness”, so audit logs, risk classification, and documentation workflows should be productized.
