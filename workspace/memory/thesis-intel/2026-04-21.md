# Thesis Intel — 2026-04-21

## TL;DR
- “Document AI” winners are the teams who can prove extraction quality (evals) and provide auditable pipelines.
- Voice agents are moving from demo to production around streaming, latency budgets, and safety/consent controls.
- Secure RAG is converging on the same enterprise primitives: redaction, audit logs, data residency, and policy-as-code.

## Hallazgos
1) docTR (open-source OCR) remains a strong baseline for document OCR pipelines
   - Why it matters: A reproducible OCR baseline helps quantify improvements from LLM-based extraction vs classic OCR.
   - Links: https://github.com/mindee/doctr
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Build an evaluation harness where every policy PDF runs through docTR first, then LLM extraction, compare deltas.

2) LayoutLMv3 (document understanding) is still a key reference point for layout-aware extraction
   - Why it matters: Layout-aware models set expectations for what “structure understanding” should look like (forms, tables).
   - Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08387
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Use a layout-aware pass for “where is the field” (regions), then LLM for “what is the field”.

3) Unstructured (document parsing) illustrates the emerging “document ETL” category
   - Why it matters: The market is standardizing around chunking/partitioning primitives for PDFs, emails, and HTML.
   - Links: https://github.com/Unstructured-IO/unstructured
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Adopt (or mimic) a partitioning layer that produces a deterministic intermediate JSON for auditing.

4) LlamaIndex parsing + ingestion patterns show the RAG stack direction for PDFs
   - Why it matters: Strong ingestion abstractions matter more than the vector DB choice when you have messy insurance docs.
   - Links: https://docs.llamaindex.ai/
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Define “document classes” (policy, endorsement, claim attachment) with per-class parsers and evals.

5) Whisper (and similar ASR stacks) still anchor reliable speech-to-text for voice workflows
   - Why it matters: For voice agents, ASR stability + diarization are often the bottleneck, not the LLM.
   - Links: https://github.com/openai/whisper
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Prototype a voice claim intake: ASR → PII redaction → structured summary → follow-up questions.

6) OWASP LLM guidance is becoming a practical checklist for secure AI delivery
   - Why it matters: Teams need concrete controls (prompt injection, data leakage, logging) to sell into regulated markets.
   - Links: https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Map Brokia’s roadmap items to OWASP LLM Top 10 controls, turn gaps into backlog.

## Ideas accionables para Brokia
- Implement an “extraction eval” suite (golden PDFs + expected fields + scoring) before scaling any document automation.
- Add audit-first primitives: per-document provenance, intermediate JSON, and change logs for every extraction run.
- For voice intake, treat latency budget + consent (recording, retention, deletion) as product features.
