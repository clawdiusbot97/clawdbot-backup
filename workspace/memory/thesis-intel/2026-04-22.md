# Thesis Intel — 2026-04-22

## TL;DR (3 bullets)
- “Agentic” enterprise LLMs are converging on built-in tool use and desktop/workflow operation, and that shifts the compliance and governance center of gravity.
- Export-controls and “cloud access as control surface” are becoming first-order constraints for frontier AI capabilities.
- Cost, evaluation, and reliability are increasingly driven by systems techniques (early exit, training loops, distributional evaluation), not only bigger models.

## Hallazgos (6)
1) GPT-5.4 positioned as an enterprise agentic model (Fortune, 2026-03-05)
   - Why it matters for the thesis: Broker/insurtech copilots that operate tools (CRMs, policy admin, spreadsheets) need the same enterprise primitives: auditability, permissioning, and predictable cost.
   - Links: https://fortune.com/2026/03/05/openai-new-model-gpt5-4-enterprise-agentic-anthropic/
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Define a “tool permission manifest” per workflow (quote, renewal, FNOL) and log every tool call + input/output hashes.

2) US Select Committee report: chip smuggling + “model distillation”, and proposed legislative levers (Select Committee on China, 2026-04-22)
   - Why it matters for the thesis: If cloud access and chip flows tighten, cost/availability becomes a product risk, especially for workloads that process sensitive documents/voice at scale.
   - Links: https://chinaselectcommittee.house.gov/media/press-releases/select-committee-investigation-reveals-china-s-history-of-ai-chip-smuggling-and-model-distillation
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Add a “model/provider fallback matrix” (by residency, latency, cost) and a per-client data residency switch.

3) Two-dimensional early-exit optimization for LLM inference (arXiv:2604.18592, 2026-04)
   - Why it matters for the thesis: Document/voice pipelines have many micro-decisions (route, validate, detect anomalies) where early-exit can materially cut spend.
   - Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18592
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Build a cheap “triage classifier layer” to decide when to escalate to heavyweight extraction or human review.

4) EasyRL: data-efficient RL using easy labeled data + progressive pseudo-labeling (arXiv:2604.18639, 2026-04)
   - Why it matters for the thesis: Insurance domains often lack labels; this suggests a pragmatic loop to improve reasoning without expensive annotation.
   - Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18639
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Start with a small labeled set of “good/bad” extraction decisions, then expand with consistency checks + reviewer-in-the-loop for medium uncertainty.

5) GROVE: visualize distributions of LM generations (arXiv:2604.18724, 2026-04)
   - Why it matters for the thesis: Extraction and customer messaging fail on edge cases. Distribution-level tooling helps find modes and brittle prompt regions earlier.
   - Links: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18724
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: For each critical prompt (coverage summary, exclusions), store 20-sample generation snapshots and surface drift across model updates.

6) Codex-style enterprise automation via plugins, memory, and multi-day agents (Big Hat Group analysis, 2026-04)
   - Why it matters for the thesis: Long-running agents amplify risk (state, memory, permissions). Insurance workflows are long-horizon by nature (renewals, endorsements, claims timelines).
   - Links: https://www.bighatgroup.com/blog/openai-codex-enterprise-ai-automation-april-2026/
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: Treat “memory” as a governed datastore: retention windows, per-field PII flags, and an audit trail for what memory influenced which decision.

## Ideas accionables para Brokia (3–5)
- Implementar “policy-as-code” para agentes: permisos por herramienta, scopes por cliente, y audit logs obligatorios.
- Triage barato primero: clasificar complejidad/risgo del documento o llamada, y solo escalar a extracción completa cuando vale la pena.
- QA por distribución: muestrear múltiples generaciones por prompt crítico, detectar modos, y versionar resultados por modelo.
- Matriz de proveedores/modelos por residencia y costo para reducir riesgo regulatorio.
