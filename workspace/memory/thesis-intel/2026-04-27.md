# Thesis Intel — 2026-04-27

## TL;DR (3 bullets)
- Benchmarks are converging on “agent-grade” document parsing (not just OCR), pushing teams to treat extraction like software with tests.
- Voice/deepfake risk is shifting from novelty to core KYC/claims threat models, with watermarking and identity verification becoming operational requirements.
- Policy friction (export controls) continues to shape AI infra costs and availability, affecting build-vs-buy decisions for regulated workloads.

## Hallazgos (6–10)
1) OCRBench v2 (multimodal OCR eval) is raising the bar on localization/handwriting/logic
   - Why it matters for the thesis: helps choose safer VLM stacks for claims/policy ingestion and prevents silent accuracy regressions.
   - Links: https://99franklin.github.io/ocrbench_v2/
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: add a “doc regression pack” (20–50 pages) and track OCRBench-style sub-metrics per vendor/model.

2) Omni benchmark repo focuses on Document → OCR → Extraction evaluation loops
   - Why it matters: aligns evaluation with real pipelines (OCR output quality impacts downstream extraction and RAG correctness).
   - Links: https://github.com/getomni-ai/benchmark
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: implement a pipeline scorecard (OCR quality + JSON schema exact match + table fidelity) as a gate for releases.

3) AudioMarkNet (audio watermarking) for proactive deepfake speech detection
   - Why it matters: watermarking can shift detection from reactive forensics to a measurable compliance control for voice workflows.
   - Links: https://www.usenix.org/system/files/usenixsecurity25-zong.pdf
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: if voice agents are in scope, require watermark presence checks on inbound audio for high-risk intents (payments, identity, claim changes).

4) WEF report on deepfakes and digital identity verification (KYC threat model)
   - Why it matters: frames deepfakes as systemic risk for digital onboarding, not just a security edge case.
   - Links: https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Unmasking_Cybercrime_Strengthening_Digital_Identity_Verification_against_Deepfakes_2026.pdf
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: define “step-up verification” triggers (voice anomaly, device mismatch, high-value action) and log evidence for audits.

5) Orange Business adds deepfake detection to enterprise communications portfolio
   - Why it matters: indicates market pull for packaged deepfake controls inside comms stacks (contact center, collaboration, CX).
   - Links: https://www.orange-business.com/en/press/newsblog-Orange-business-adds-deepfake-detection-enterprise-communications
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: partner/plug-in approach: integrate a deepfake detection API as a modular component in claims intake.

6) CRS report: U.S. export controls and China (advanced semiconductors)
   - Why it matters: policy changes can alter compute availability and pricing, especially for firms relying on specific accelerators or regions.
   - Links: https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48642/R48642.5.pdf
   - Actionable idea for Brokia: build an infra risk register (vendor, region, accelerator class) and pre-approve an alternate deployment plan for regulated data.

## Ideas accionables para Brokia (3–5)
- “Doc Quality Gate”: automatic regression suite for doc parsing/extraction before shipping changes (benchmark-like scorecard).
- “Audit-ready Intake”: capture structured evidence for identity/claim actions (what model, confidence, step-up checks triggered).
- “Voice Risk Control Pack”: watermark check + anomaly detection + escalation workflow for high-risk calls.
- “Compute Resilience”: minimize coupling to one accelerator/vendor by supporting at least 2 deployment backends.
